Derived asset: baby-yoda.bin
Method: deterministic area-weighted triangle-surface sampling (18,000 points), after applying
embedded GLB node transforms followed by the Voyager model and scene-node transforms. The sampled
bounds are centred, uniformly scaled and turned around the upright axis for the landing artwork,
then quantized to signed 16-bit xyz.
The source GLB and its textures are not distributed with this derived point cloud.

Orange highlights in the landing artwork are conceptual interaction accents. They are not sampled
from, and do not represent, annotation coordinates in the source edition.
