Derived asset: petrol-lamp.bin
Method: deterministic area-weighted triangle-surface sampling (18,000 points) from the lamp's
100,000 triangles. The unrelated plane and tube scene meshes are intentionally excluded. Embedded
GLB node transforms are applied before the Voyager model and scene-node transforms. The sampled
bounds are centred, uniformly fitted to the landing artwork, turned around the upright axis, and
quantized to signed 16-bit xyz. The source GLB and its textures are not distributed with this
derived point cloud.

Orange highlights in the landing artwork are conceptual interaction accents. They are not sampled
from, and do not represent, annotation coordinates in the source edition.
