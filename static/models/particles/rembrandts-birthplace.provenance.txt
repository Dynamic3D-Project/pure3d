Derived asset: rembrandts-birthplace.bin
Method: deterministic area-weighted triangle-surface sampling (18,000 points) from the two
SchaalMiddenHuis building primitives and 1,360 triangles. The other houses, grass, ground, garden,
tree and loose scene-item meshes are intentionally excluded so the birthplace's gabled building
remains legible. Embedded GLB node transforms are applied before the Voyager model and scene-node
transforms. The sampled bounds are centred, uniformly fitted to the landing artwork, turned to the
published view's oblique architectural angle, and quantized to signed 16-bit xyz. The source GLB
and its textures are not distributed with this derived point cloud.

Orange highlights in the landing artwork are conceptual interaction accents. They are not sampled
from, and do not represent, annotation coordinates in the source edition.
