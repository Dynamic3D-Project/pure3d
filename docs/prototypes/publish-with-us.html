Publish with Pure3D

Give your 3D research a scholarly home.

Bring a 3D object, its evidence, and your interpretation together in one edition designed to be explored—not flattened into figures.

What authors publish

More than a model.

A Pure3D edition makes a research object understandable by keeping the model, the scholarly argument, and its supporting record in conversation.

01

A navigable 3D object

A research-ready model that readers can inspect from the viewpoints your argument requires.

02

Structured interpretation

Annotations and authored pathways connect features of the object to a clear research question.

03

Evidence and provenance

Sources, methods, rights information, and supporting media let readers assess how the edition was made.

Before you begin

A useful first conversation starts here.

You do not need a finished edition to start. You should be able to identify the core research contribution and show that the required materials can be used.

Have these ready

  • A concise research question and account of why 3D is necessary.
  • A representative model or enough source material to assess feasibility.
  • ORCID iDs for every individual author; contributor ORCIDs are optional. Organisations are listed by name.
  • Evidence that you hold, or can secure, rights and permissions for every asset.
  • Sources and documentation that support the model and interpretation.
